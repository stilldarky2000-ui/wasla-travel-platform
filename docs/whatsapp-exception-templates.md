# Wasla Travel: WhatsApp Operational Exception Templates (EN / DE)

**Companion file to `docs/whatsapp-templates.md` (which remains strictly untouched).**
**Status:** Draft for owner approval as quick-reply templates in WhatsApp Business.

Placeholders: `{guestName}` `{tripName}` `{date}` `{pickupTime}` `{newPickupTime}` `{pickupPoint}` `{bookingRef}` `{minutes}`

---

## 1. Guest Did Not Reply to Day-Before Confirmation

Sent in the evening if the guest has not replied to Template 2 (`Day-before confirmation`).

EN: Hi {guestName}, just following up for your {tripName} tomorrow, {date}. Our driver is scheduled for {pickupTime} at the {pickupPoint}. Please reply "OK" here so we can confirm your seat and pickup with the driver. Ref: {bookingRef}.

DE: Hallo {guestName}, eine kurze Nachfrage zu deinem Ausflug {tripName} morgen, am {date}. Dein Fahrer ist für {pickupTime} am {pickupPoint} eingeplant. Bitte antworte kurz mit "OK", damit wir deinen Platz und die Abholung beim Fahrer fest bestätigen können. Ref: {bookingRef}.

---

## 2. Pickup / Driver Delay

Sent immediately if traffic, road checks, or an earlier hotel pickup delays the driver.

EN: Hi {guestName}, quick update on your {tripName} pickup (Ref: {bookingRef}). Our driver is delayed by about {minutes} minutes due to local traffic and will reach the {pickupPoint} around {newPickupTime}. Thank you for your patience, we are on our way.

DE: Hallo {guestName}, ein kurzes Update zu deiner Abholung für {tripName} (Ref: {bookingRef}). Unser Fahrer verspätet sich wegen des Verkehrs um etwa {minutes} Minuten und ist gegen {newPickupTime} am {pickupPoint}. Vielen Dank für deine Geduld, wir sind gleich da.

---

## 3. Guest No-Show (Two Stages)

### 3a. Driver Waiting at Pickup Point (Final Check Before Departure)

EN: Hi {guestName}, our driver is waiting at the {pickupPoint} for your {tripName} (Ref: {bookingRef}). We have not spotted you yet. Can you please message us right now or let us know if you are on your way down?

DE: Hallo {guestName}, unser Fahrer wartet am {pickupPoint} auf dich für {tripName} (Ref: {bookingRef}). Wir haben dich noch nicht gesehen. Schreib uns bitte kurz hier, ob du schon auf dem Weg nach draußen bist.

### 3b. Polite Post-Departure Follow-Up & Easy Rebooking

Sent only after the vehicle has had to depart without the guest, keeping the door open for another day.

EN: Hi {guestName}, our driver waited at the {pickupPoint} for your {tripName} today ({date}, Ref: {bookingRef}) and had to depart with the group. We hope everything is okay. Nothing was charged, and if you would like to reschedule for another day during your stay, just message us here.

DE: Hallo {guestName}, unser Fahrer hat heute ({date}, Ref: {bookingRef}) am {pickupPoint} für {tripName} gewartet und musste leider mit der Gruppe abfahren. Wir hoffen, bei dir ist alles in Ordnung. Es wurde nichts berechnet. Wenn du den Ausflug an einem anderen Tag nachholen möchtest, schreib uns einfach kurz hier.
