## 3. Testimonials (the 5 real Luxor-trip texts the owner supplied)

1. The qualifier label travels with EVERY testimonial (each carousel slide carries it, not only a section header). Section heading per the copy expert: "Guests from the operator's earlier tours", with the subline: "Wasla is new. The man running your trip is not. These are words from guests he took out before Wasla existed." Each item labelled by trip + guest language, no names.
2. No stars, no scores, no counts, no aggregate language anywhere near them.
3. Framing names the person, not the platform; testimonials never migrate into trip pages or near prices.
4. Consent proof on file BEFORE publication: dated screenshots of the consent messages, stored with this package.
5. Verbatim or nothing; never silently translate a quote (a translation must be marked as one).
If condition 1 or 2 cannot hold in the rendered implementation, the section does not publish.
