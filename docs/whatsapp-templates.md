# Wasla guest message templates (EN/DE) — final set, 2026-11-24

Drafted by an external model from Muse's brief, reviewed line by line by Muse, template 1 rewritten by Muse in the guest's voice and confirmed by AHMED 2026-11-24. Templates 2–5 as delivered. Note: Muse flagged the free-cancellation line in template 2 as unnecessary the day before (the 24h deadline has usually passed by then); AHMED kept templates 2–5 as delivered.

Placeholders: {guestName} {tripName} {date} {pickupTime} {pickupPoint} {guests} {totalPrice} {bookingRef} {hotelName} {reviewLink}
Site note: the WhatsApp pre-fill for template 1 needs {hotelName} from the booking form's hotel field.

## 1. Post-booking message (guest sends us right after booking)

EN: Hello Wasla Travel, I have just booked on your website. Booking reference {bookingRef}: {tripName} on {date}, {guests} guests, total {totalPrice}, cash on pickup. My name is {guestName} and my hotel is {hotelName}. Please confirm my booking and my pickup time. Thank you.

DE: Hallo Wasla Travel, ich habe gerade auf eurer Website gebucht. Buchungsreferenz {bookingRef}: {tripName} am {date}, {guests} Gäste, Gesamtpreis {totalPrice}, Bezahlung in bar bei der Abholung. Mein Name ist {guestName}, mein Hotel ist {hotelName}. Bitte bestätigt meine Buchung und meine Abholzeit. Vielen Dank.

## 2. Day-before confirmation (we send; carries the confirmed pickup time)

EN: Hi {guestName}, a quick reminder that your {tripName} is tomorrow, {date}. Our local team will pick you up at {pickupTime} from the {pickupPoint} at your hotel. Please reply to this message to confirm so the driver knows to wait for you. Bring your booking reference {bookingRef}, sunscreen, and a bit of cash for the payment. Please reply to this message to confirm for tomorrow. If you cannot join us, please tell us tonight.

DE: Hallo {guestName}, kurze Erinnerung: Dein Ausflug {tripName} findet morgen, am {date}, statt. Unser lokales Team holt dich um {pickupTime} am {pickupPoint} deines Hotels ab. Bitte bestätige kurz hier im Chat, damit der Fahrer Bescheid weiß. Denke an deine Buchungsreferenz {bookingRef}, Sonnenschutz und etwas Bargeld für die Bezahlung. Bitte antworten Sie auf diese Nachricht und bestätigen Sie für morgen. Falls Sie nicht teilnehmen können, sagen Sie uns bitte heute Abend Bescheid.

## 3. Pickup-morning message

EN: Good morning {guestName}, your driver is on the way. Please be ready at the {pickupPoint} at {pickupTime}. If anything changes, message us right here.

DE: Guten Morgen {guestName}, dein Fahrer ist unterwegs. Bitte sei pünktlich um {pickupTime} am {pickupPoint}. Wenn sich etwas ändert, schreib uns einfach hier.

## 4. Pickup-point message

EN: Hi {guestName}, small note for your {tripName} on {date}. Your hotel has a fixed meeting point, so our local team will wait for you at the {pickupPoint} at exactly {pickupTime}. Please be there a few minutes early. If you cannot find us, message us here and we will help right away.

DE: Hallo {guestName}, ein kurzer Hinweis zu deinem Ausflug {tripName} am {date}. Dein Hotel hat einen festen Treffpunkt, unser lokales Team wartet deshalb um {pickupTime} am {pickupPoint} auf dich. Sei bitte ein paar Minuten früher da. Falls du uns nicht findest, schreib uns einfach hier, wir helfen sofort weiter.

## 5. Review request (after the trip)

EN: Hi {guestName}, thank you for spending the day with Wasla Travel. We hope you enjoyed the {tripName}. If you have a moment, a Google review helps our small local team a lot: {reviewLink}. Thanks so much, and we hope to see you again in Hurghada.

DE: Hallo {guestName}, danke, dass du den Tag mit Wasla Travel verbracht hast. Wir hoffen, der {tripName} hat dir gefallen. Wenn du kurz Zeit hast, hilft uns eine Bewertung bei Google sehr, unser kleines Team freut sich darüber: {reviewLink}. Vielen Dank und hoffentlich bis bald in Hurghada.
